This section will list all of the major changes that happened with the release of **Momentum 5.3.1**. Depending on installation type, all changes may not be applicable

<a name="changelog.5.3.1.table"></a>

| Type | Ticket | Description |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Enhancement | I-1651 | The **experimental** [DKIM2](/momentum/4/dkim2) module now implements the **-06** draft version of the specification. Because what is hashed and signed changed, signatures do not verify between Momentum 5.3.0 and 5.3.1 in either direction for Ed25519 signatures, for messages with a body line starting with `.`, and for messages carrying a header field whose hashing changed, such as `Received-SPF`. |
| Feature | I-1651 | DKIM2 [signing](/momentum/4/dkim2/sign) now refuses, by default, a recipe whose header-field keys are not lower case, a `mi_hash_algorithms` list naming anything other than `sha256` or `sha512` or naming one twice, and more than two `sig_sets` for one algorithm; policy scripts that did this under 5.3.0 fail to sign after the upgrade. [Verification](/momentum/4/dkim2/verify) returns `permerror` for a recipe key that is not lower case, for a `Message-Instance` naming `sha256` or `sha512` twice, and for a signature carrying more than two sig-sets for one algorithm. The new `on_recipe_key_case` and `on_bad_hash_algorithm` options, on both, accept the recipe-key and hash-algorithm cases instead. |
